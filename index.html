About

00 / INDEX

Om — working across graphic design, art and philosophy, with a long-running practice in music and a habit of building things in code. Interested in the seams between disciplines: where a record cover is an argument, where a trading model is a kind of writing.

Practice

I treat design and code as the same act from two angles — both give form to something that didn't have one. Most of what I make starts as a question I can't stop turning over.

Visual language: brutalist, clean, deliberate. Type that knows what it's doing, space that earns its keep, nothing decorative for its own sake.

Currently

Building software for non-technical people, shooting on the side, and listening to a lot of plugg. Available for collaborations and good arguments.

Philosophy

02 / IDEAS
Nothing matters — and that is the matter.

I hold a nihilism that doesn't end in despair. Yes: nothing carries meaning handed down from outside — no cosmic ledger, no built-in purpose. But I don't treat that as the conclusion. I treat it as the first fact of being alive.

That nothing matters is itself a matter of life in-itself. The absence of given meaning isn't a void to fall into; it's the open ground we get to build on. Meaninglessness is the condition for freedom, not its enemy.

So I make things anyway — music, design, code — not because they're cosmically important, but because the making is the meaning. Value isn't discovered; it's authored. The universe's indifference is exactly what hands the pen back to you.

If nothing is required of you, then everything is permitted to you. That's not nothing. That's the whole thing.
